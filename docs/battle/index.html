HOW TO FIGHT

Three bots, in order. Scrap Rover, Heavy Mech, then the Omega Core. Each fight lasts 60 seconds. Run out of health or time and you retry the stage.

Attack (A or J) is a quick punch. Shield (hold S or K) blocks most of a hit. Raise it just before the hit lands for a parry: no damage, the enemy staggers and you gain energy. Charge (D or L) fills one energy cell, but a hit cancels it. Overdrive (F or ;) costs 2 energy: a beam that breaks guards and stops a wind-up cold.

Read the tells. A red eye and a raised arm mean a heavy hit. Plates up mean a guard: attacks bounce. Steam means it is venting: it takes double damage. A white ring means the crusher: a shield only takes half of it.

Same fight every time. Every bot follows a fixed rhythm and the game runs at a fixed 60 ticks a second, so you can learn it. The Omega Core changes rhythm twice as it breaks.
